# Recruiter licensing

Status: Research brief for counsel and partner interviews

Last updated: 2026-07-17

## What “licensed recruiter” means

Recruitment authorization belongs to a jurisdiction. The government grants a business permission to accept vacancies, accept candidates, arrange employment relationships, and collect specified fees. The regulator may also set capital, office, staffing, privacy, advertising, reporting, bond, fee, and complaint rules.

Four permissions can touch one JobKit placement:

1. JobKit's home jurisdiction governs its company, candidate agreement, coaching revenue, overseas sourcing, privacy, and fee structure.
1. The destination country governs local employment placement, local fee collection, and school introductions.
1. The destination's immigration and education authorities govern the school, work authorization, teacher credential, and sponsor process.
1. A staffing model adds payroll, labor-dispatch, insurance, tax, and employer obligations.

The practical pilot structure is simple: JobKit sources and prepares candidates; a licensed destination partner owns the local school agreement, regulated introduction, visa handoff, and placement invoice.

## United States home-jurisdiction review

The entity state needs a written legal classification before launch. The key facts are candidate fees, access to specific vacancies, employer contact, success fees, and marketing promises.

Washington provides a concrete example. The [Department of Licensing](https://dol.wa.gov/professional-licenses/employment-agencies/forms-employment-agencies) tells applicants to call before filing. Its current published requirements include a $2,000 bond or deposit, a licensed general manager, a manager examination, and office licensing. The current [fee page](https://dol.wa.gov/professional-licenses/employment-agencies/fees-employment-agencies) lists $823 for the main office and $150 for the manager exam. [RCW 19.31](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=19.31&full=true) controls the final classification.

A clean contract stack helps counsel draw the lines:

- candidate training agreement;
- candidate relocation and application-management agreement;
- school placement agreement;
- destination-partner sourcing agreement; and
- privacy and deletion schedule.

## Korea

**Authorization:** registered fee-charging domestic job-placement service.

**Regulator:** Ministry of Employment and Labor rules, with registration at the city, county, or district governing the Korean office.

**Core requirements for one corporate office:**

- Korean qualifying company;
- ₩50 million paid-in capital, about $33,300;
- two qualified corporate officers;
- one qualified vocational counselor;
- dedicated office of at least 10 square metres;
- ₩10 million deposit or guarantee insurance, about $6,660; and
- ₩30,000 registration application fee, about $20.

The [Employment Security Act](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1024682547), [current registration requirements](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lumLsLinkPop.do?lspttninfSeq=81969), and [deposit rule](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?ancYnChk=0&chrClsCd=010202&efYd=20260324&lsiSeq=284877&urlMode=lsInfoP) supply the governing details.

Korean law caps employer fees for contracts of at least three months at roughly 7.5% of annual salary. [Korvia publishes](https://www.korvia.com/fee-disclosure-en) its registration number, fee schedule, payment triggers, and guarantee. [Planet ESL](https://www.planetesl.com/services-for-employers/) and [OK Recruiting](https://www.okrecruiting.com/about-us/) also publish recruiter registration details.

**JobKit route:** licensed Korean partner first. A Korean entity becomes sensible after recurring volume supports local capital, qualified officers, a counselor, office operations, and school sales.

**Candidate gate:** the standard E-2/EPIK lane requires an accredited bachelor’s degree, eligible citizenship and education history, and the correct visa. EPIK also specifies TEFL requirements for many applicants. [EPIK eligibility](https://www.epik.go.kr/web/epik/contents/Eligibility) is the pre-screening source. JobKit's training can satisfy preparation and a recognized TEFL requirement; degree, citizenship, and visa eligibility come from the candidate's underlying record.

## Japan

**Authorization:** paid employment-placement permit.

**Regulator:** Minister of Health, Labour and Welfare through the prefectural Labour Bureau.

**Core requirements for one office:**

- ¥5 million net assets, about $31,000;
- ¥1.5 million cash and deposits, about $9,300;
- responsible placement manager with qualifying experience and approved training;
- privacy-capable office or qualifying internet-only operating arrangement;
- ¥50,000 permit fee plus ¥90,000 registration tax, about $868 combined; and
- application roughly three months before launch.

The [Osaka Labour Bureau](https://jsite.mhlw.go.jp/osaka-roudoukyoku/hourei_seido_tetsuzuki/yuryou_muryou_shokugyou/hourei_seido/kyoka.html) publishes the criteria. The [MHLW international-placement model](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/11650000/001280505.pdf) defines how a licensed Japanese agency can work with an overseas intermediary.

Japan also has a separate worker-dispatch license for the Actalent-style model. [Global Partners](https://www.globalpartners.co.jp/service-en/recruiting-en/) publicly offers direct referral and teacher dispatch. [MEXT's FY2023 ALT survey](https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20240527-mxt_kyoiku01-000035833_1.pdf) found 34.1% of participating ALTs supplied through dispatch contracts.

**JobKit route:** overseas sourcing intermediary to a licensed Japanese teacher agency. Direct-hire referral comes first. Dispatch follows after Japanese payroll, supervision, visa, procurement, and substitute coverage become operational.

**Candidate gate:** public and formal-school Instructor status generally uses a degree, qualifying education, or teaching license. Private-company language instruction can use a degree or three years of relevant professional experience. [Instructor criteria](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001367639.pdf) and [International Services criteria](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001366995.pdf) control the lane.

Japan gives JobKit a useful product rule. [MHLW guidance](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/general/seido/anteikyoku/syoukai/syoukai1.html) allows voluntary candidate-paid education and related services when their purchase and delivery stay clearly separate from employment placement. This supports independent training, interview coaching, document help, and relocation planning.

## China

**Authorization:** Human Resources Service License.

**Regulator:** local human-resources and social-security authority for the Chinese entity.

The national [Human Resources Service Institution Management Provisions](https://chinajob.mohrss.gov.cn/h5/c/2023-07-03/381610.shtml) cover worker recommendations, employer introductions, job fairs, online recruitment, and executive search. Core requirements include a Chinese market entity, operating rules, a fixed place, facilities, startup funds, and at least three full-time staff. Local offices add application detail. A wholly foreign-owned recruiting company is legally possible; the pilot gains far more from a proven local partner's school relationships, contracting, work-permit experience, and collections.

China's Actalent-style route is labor dispatch. The [labor-dispatch licensing measures](https://www.mohrss.gov.cn/xxgk2020/gzk/gz/202112/P020211229556086979643.pdf) require CNY 2 million paid-in capital, about $294,300, plus ongoing employer and payroll duties. Direct placement offers a far lighter first business.

**JobKit route:** supply screened candidates to two licensed Chinese recruiters. The recruiter verifies the school, owns local placement and work-permit execution, and pays JobKit after 30-45 completed workdays.

**Candidate gate:** the standard foreign-language-teacher lane requires a bachelor’s degree. A teaching credential or accepted TEFL/TESOL/CELTA can replace the usual two-year experience requirement. [Beijing](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/government/policytoolkittwo/INV_Policy_Text/202411/t20241118_3944100.html) and [Tianjin](https://en.tj.gov.cn/services/ForeignServiceChecklist/WorkPermit/GUIDELINES_FOR_WORK_PERMITS/CHAPTER_TWO_EVALUATION_CRITERIA/202403/t20240301_6549822.html) publish the criteria. For this market, the TEFL issuer needs a proven authentication and work-permit record.

## Thailand

**Authorization:** domestic employment-arrangement license.

**Regulator:** Department of Employment, Ministry of Labour.

The [Employment Arrangement and Jobseeker Protection Act](https://www.doe.go.th/prd/assets/upload/files/legal_th/ad44f8872b422773e61ef678e2221289.pdf) and [current application procedure](https://www.doe.go.th/prd/download/download_by_pool_file/13458) require a Thai-national licensee or Thai legal person, Thai manager, THB 100,000 security, physical office of at least 16 square metres, THB 5,000 fee, and a two-year province-specific license. Registered employment agents add THB 50,000 security each. Advertising approval and monthly reports form part of operations.

**JobKit route:** genuine independent Thai licensed partner. JobKit supplies degree-qualified, document-ready candidates and technology. The local partner handles the regulated introduction and school-side fee.

**Candidate gate:** the standard formal-school lane uses a bachelor’s degree, lawful visa status, work permit, and a school-specific Teachers’ Council permission or professional license. Thailand's [foreign-teacher permission guide](https://thailand.go.th/issue-focus-detail/001_01_235) explains the school-specific route.

## Colombia

**Authorization:** employment-placement authorization from the Servicio Público de Empleo. Transnational placement adds operating procedures, background checks, and compliance insurance.

The [authorization guidance](https://www.serviciodeempleo.gov.co/atencion-a-la-ciudadania/tramite-en-linea/preguntas-frecuentes-tramites-de-autorizacion-o-modificacion/) and [transnational operating guide](https://www.serviciodeempleo.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/GR-In-04-Guia-para-la-Elaboracion-del-Esquema-de-Operacion-Transnacional_6.pdf) define the process.

**JobKit route:** authorized Colombian partner for the first school placements. The M Worker visa uses an employment contract, employer justification, employer solvency, and relevant candidate education or experience. [Colombia's visa resolution](https://www.cancilleria.gov.co/normograma/compilacion/docs/pdf/resolucion_minrelaciones_5477_2022.pdf) supplies the formal rules.

## Georgia, Europe, Brazil, Kazakhstan, and Russia

- **Georgia:** employer-specific work authorization has applied since March 1, 2026. A sponsor-ready school files after contract signature. Local counsel should classify the recruiting activity. [Work-right law](https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/2806732?fullscreen=1&impose=parallelEn)
- **European Union:** candidate work rights and destination-country agency rules create distinct products. EU/EEA work-right candidates, formal assistant programs, international schools, and sponsored vacancies each deserve their own lane. [EU work-right overview](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/work/work-abroad/work-permits/index_en.htm)
- **Brazil:** the school files the labor-residence application. International and bilingual schools offer the useful economic lane. [Brazil labor residence](https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/obter-autorizacao-de-residencia-para-fins-laborais-a-imigrantes)
- **Kazakhstan:** the employer generally handles a foreign-labor permit and C3 visa. International schools and exempt education organizations offer the useful lane. [Kazakhstan foreign-worker rules](https://egov.kz/cms/en/articles/inostrannyi_trud)
- **Russia:** active placement stays at the research desk. The [U.S. State Department advisory](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/russia.html) identifies wrongful-detention and consular exposure; [OFAC](https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-programs-and-country-information/russian-harmful-foreign-activities-sanctions) identifies sanctions and payment exposure.

## License acquisition threshold

Own licensing becomes attractive when one destination reaches all five conditions:

1. fifty or more annual placements with verified starts and early retention;
1. at least three recurring school groups buying placements every semester;
1. a local operating lead who can satisfy regulator and school requirements;
1. twelve months of predictable partner economics; and
1. legal review showing higher contribution after office, capital, staff, compliance, and insurance.

Until then, a partner share buys a functioning school network and regulated local execution.
