## Evidence labels

- **Official:** statute, regulator, immigration authority, school, or government dataset.
- **Commercial:** provider-published fee, guarantee, service, or partner program.
- **Inventory:** current JobKit source database.
- **Proposal:** price or operating term for direct market testing.
- **Inference:** conclusion drawn across sources and labeled in the research brief.

## Marketing and safety precedents

- [USIT](https://www.usit.ie/) uses the direct emotional hook “Need to get out of here?” and sells structured working-holiday, J-1, camp, visa, banking, tax, and job support.
- [NNEDV confidentiality guidance](https://www.techsafety.org/confidentiality) supports minimal collection and clear access rules.
- [NNEDV retention guidance](https://www.techsafety.org/retention) supports defined deletion schedules.
- [National Domestic Violence Hotline safety plan](https://safety-plan.thehotline.org/) demonstrates device-local planning and a quick-exit pattern.
- [DOJ extradition guidance](https://www.justice.gov/jm/jm-9-15000-international-extradition-and-related-matters) shows why legal-process questions require licensed counsel and case-specific facts.

## Claims that require direct validation

Partner interviews should establish:

- sourcing share or flat fee;
- payment trigger;
- attribution period;
- replacement allocation;
- candidate and school ownership;
- document-storage permissions;
- school verification method;
- immigration handoff;
- average annual placement volume;
- early-departure causes; and
- partner license status.

These answers turn public research into a contract and operating model.
