## Korea

Korea offers the cleanest first unit. Schools already use placement agencies, a public ₩1.5 million hagwon fee benchmark exists, housing and visa coordination form part of the service, and candidate eligibility is easy to pre-screen.

The standard candidate file should include:

- eligible citizenship and English-language education history;
- accredited bachelor's degree;
- criminal-check and document status;
- E-2 route;
- TEFL status where the program requires it;
- preferred cities, age groups, schedule, salary, housing, and start date;
- recorded screen; and
- short teaching demonstration.

The first business objective is three starts through one licensed Korean partner, followed by one hagwon agreement billed through that partner.

## China

China has the richest published partner economics. Established agencies publicly offer CNY 2,000-6,000, about $294-$883, for successful referral partners. One program pays after 45 completed teaching days. Retail placement disclosures run from CNY 12,000 to CNY 30,000, about $1,766-$4,415.

This directly answers whether agencies want outside recruiters: several invite companies and individuals to supply teachers, use tracked referrals, and pay after verified employment.

The standard China candidate file should include:

- bachelor's degree;
- nationality and mother-tongue fit;
- authenticated degree and criminal-check path;
- accepted teaching credential or experience route;
- salary and city range;
- legal work-permit review; and
- 30-45 day retention commitment.

The first offer to a Chinese recruiter is CNY 4,000-6,000 per retained teacher. The partner owns the school, contract, work permit, and collection. JobKit owns sourcing, preparation, candidate communication, and retention follow-up.

## Thailand

Thailand presents both placement and managed staffing. Direct private schools, public procurement, and Thai agencies all buy teacher supply. MediaKids describes a managed model spanning recruitment, training, curriculum, housing help, visa support, and school coordination.

The reliable formal-school lane uses a bachelor's degree, school sponsorship, work permit, and Teachers' Council permission or license. Candidate preparation can include TEFL and observed practice while the school-specific authorization controls legal teaching.

The first wholesale offer is THB 7,500-15,000, about $224-$448, after first payroll or 30 completed days. A later direct-school offer through a licensed Thai entity can charge THB 30,000-40,000, about $895-$1,194, with a 60-day replacement.

Thailand is also the best place to study the Actalent-style model. Municipalities and schools procure a complete foreign-teacher service. The agency recruits teachers, manages payroll, coordinates permits, reports attendance, handles substitutes, and replaces departures. This creates recurring revenue and working-capital needs.

## Japan

Japan supports direct referral and worker dispatch. Public-school ALT procurement feeds large dispatch businesses; private language schools, preschools, bilingual schools, and private secondary schools support direct-hire referral.

The first route uses a licensed Japanese teacher agency. JobKit supplies overseas candidates with degree or three-year experience evidence, language-education history, visa-lane analysis, teaching demonstration, and document status.

The proposed entry price is ¥500,000, about $3,100, per teacher after the first workday, with 90-day replacement coverage. A competitor describes a broader foreign-worker market at 20-35% of annual salary; a ¥3.6 million salary would yield about $4,460-$7,810.

Public ALT dispatch belongs to a later Japanese company with a dispatch license, payroll, insurance, substitute coverage, supervision, and procurement capability.

## Colombia and Latin America

Colombia is the strongest expansion market outside East Asia. Its M Worker visa uses an employment contract, employer justification, solvency evidence, and candidate education or experience. An authorized transnational placement partner should handle the regulated local layer.

Current specialist listings show packages around:

- $2,794 salary plus $279 housing in Cali;
- $1,894 salary plus $341 housing in Santa Marta; and
- $1,552 salary plus $528 housing in Pereira.

These packages can support a $1,000-$2,000 school-paid fee. The first client type is a bilingual or international school already budgeting for visa, flight, or housing.

Broader Latin American international schools form a separate qualified-teacher lane. AMISA represents more than 65 schools across 25 countries, while Search Associates publishes a $1,980 school fee for each teacher or intern placed. This lane serves teaching-license holders and experienced classroom educators.

Brazil fits this qualified-school lane. Local language-school and freelance rates create thinner placement economics, while bilingual and international schools can fund sponsorship and recruitment.

## Georgia, Spain, and broader Europe

Georgia has real direct hiring and a new employer-specific work-authorization process. Current examples pay roughly $960-$1,230 monthly. A sponsor-ready school pilot can support a small flat success fee.

Spain already has a structured language-assistant route. Meddeas places assistants into private and charter schools, supports visas, and publishes €465-€1,445 monthly compensation. This model points toward a school-program partnership plus a candidate-side preparation and relocation service.

Europe should be sold as four specific lanes:

- EU/EEA candidates exercising existing work rights;
- formal language-assistant programs;
- credentialed teachers entering international schools; and
- employer-sponsored vacancies tied to a specific national permit.

## Degree-free routes

A private TEFL certificate has real training value. Its immigration value depends on each route.

- Japan offers a narrow private-company language-instruction lane through three years of relevant professional experience.
- South American language institutes may hire candidates who already hold local work rights.
- EU/EEA candidates can use their existing work rights across many European markets.
- Korean E-2, standard Chinese foreign-language teaching, and formal Thai school routes center on a bachelor's degree.

JobKit should show the route first, then attach the right credential. A detailed readiness record can strengthen every application: hours completed, live instruction, observed practice, teaching demonstration, trainer feedback, and interview performance.
