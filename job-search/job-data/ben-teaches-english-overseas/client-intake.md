## Contract Review Request

The contract was an English First offer in China. Chibuzor's completed form recorded:

- The offer itself was the main positive; there was not yet a strong positive case for the specific
  package.
- The salary felt low relative to previous US employment.
- Weekend work, limited vacation, and the overall time commitment were concerns.
- Calculated net hourly pay: about 80 CNY per hour.
- No negotiation had occurred yet.
- The main desired negotiation was salary, with uncertainty about realistic leverage.
- Chibuzor believed English First was the direct employer.
- Quoted net salary was 9,000 CNY during probation and 10,000 CNY afterward.
- The overall gut reaction was that the offer was low, tempered by uncertainty about being new to
  ESL work.
- Free Mandarin lessons were verbally mentioned.
- Housing or a housing allowance was verbally mentioned, but the allowance amount was not visible
  in the contract and the provided housing had been described negatively.

These answers explain the contract Ben later said to "run away from." They are distinct from the
16,000 RMB package discussed in August 2019.
