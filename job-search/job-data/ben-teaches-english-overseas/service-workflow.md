# Service Workflow and Deliverables

This is a cleaned Markdown conversion of the operational Ben Teaches English Overseas messages in
the raw email archive. It records what the purchased service included and how Ben asked clients to
use it. It is historical source material, not current JobKit policy.

The June 3 and June 6 instruction messages are substantively identical. Account credentials,
payment details, coupon details, unsubscribe links, and signature marketing have been omitted.

## Coaching workflow

- The package included coaching by email.
- Ben asked clients to keep messages reasonably focused because very long messages delayed his
  response.
- For a legitimate time-sensitive decision, such as an expiring contract or employer deadline, he
  instructed clients to put `Emergency` in the email subject.

## Resume workflow

- Read the service manual before submitting a resume unless the situation is genuinely urgent.
- Read the resume-folder `READ ME` before submitting documents.
- Complete the client questionnaire as the first step.
- The surviving archive refers to the manual and resume-folder instructions, but neither file is
  present in the JobKit or recovered OneNote material.

## Contract-review workflow

- Complete the Contract Review Request Form.
- Attach both the completed form and the contract.
- Provide the personal goals and concerns needed to evaluate the package; do not send an unexplained
  contract and expect a useful review.

## Final document delivery

2. an editable DOCX resume; and
3. an editable DOCX cover-letter template for creating employer-specific letters.

He offered to help modify the cover-letter template and correct any problems in the delivered
documents. The three files remain embedded in the raw `Your documents` email. Their filenames are:

- `Chibuzor Ejimofor.pdf`

[`cover-letter-template.md`](cover-letter-template.md). The accessible `attachments/` directory has
