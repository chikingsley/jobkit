# Ben Teaches English Overseas Archive

Local archive of Chibuzor's 2019 paid ESL career consultation with Ben Teaches English Overseas.
The original site is no longer reachable; a 2023 email requesting the ebook or manual bounced.

## Readable Markdown corpus

- [`correspondence.md`](correspondence.md) — the two substantive consultation threads: resume and
  contract advice in June 2019, then market and outreach feedback in August 2019.
- [`service-workflow.md`](service-workflow.md) — service instructions, contract-review workflow,
  final document delivery, and package end.
- [`client-intake.md`](client-intake.md) — searchable conversion of the survey, contract-review, and
  financial-goal forms.
- [`cover-letter-template.md`](cover-letter-template.md) — conversion of the editable cover-letter
  template Ben delivered.
- [`onenote-notes.md`](onenote-notes.md) — unique related notes recovered from the Last Chance at
  Life OneNote export; duplicates are identified rather than copied again.
- [`source-audit.md`](source-audit.md) — exact coverage, omissions, provenance, and missing material.

The distilled, current interpretation lives in
[`../../docs/job-search-playbook.md`](../../docs/job-search-playbook.md). That playbook explicitly
separates Ben's 2019 advice, historical artifacts, observations from Chibuzor's sent-email corpus,
and current JobKit policy.

## Primary sources

- [`emails/`](emails/) contains all 23 recovered raw `.eml` messages from the Ben-related mailbox
  threads dated 2019-2023. These files are authoritative for exact wording and thread order.
- [`attachments/`](attachments/) contains 22 accessible exchanged files: filled intake forms,
  credentials, resume drafts and design options, the finished teaching resume, the real 2019 Moscow
  outreach email, and the English First offer package.

The June 16 `Your documents` email also embeds three delivered files that are not separately exposed
under their original filenames in `attachments/`: `Chibuzor Ejimofor.pdf`,
`Chibuzor Ejimofor.docx`, and `Chibuzor Ejimofor Cover Letter Template.docx`. The cover-letter body
is preserved in Markdown. See [`source-audit.md`](source-audit.md) before making completeness claims.

The `Chi Offer Package.zip` attachment is the English First contract package, not Ben's service
manual or ebook. No recoverable copy of the manual or ebook was found in JobKit, the raw Ben email,
or the relevant OneNote page.
