## Correspondence audit

[`correspondence.md`](correspondence.md) is a useful readable transcript of the two substantive
consultation threads. It is not, and should not be described as, a complete conversion of the whole
Ben archive. It omits the purchase workflow, final document-delivery note, membership email, and the
failed 2023 ebook request. Those omissions are now covered by the other Markdown files indexed in
the README.

The transcript retains a few quoted-message markers and formatting artifacts from the original
email chain. The substance of Ben's advice is intact. The raw `.eml` files remain authoritative when
wording or thread order matters.

## Last Chance at Life / OneNote comparison

The recovered OneNote page does **not** contain the missing Ben manual or ebook. Its three embedded
PDFs match documents already in JobKit, and its copied June 4 email duplicates the raw mailbox
archive. Only the service/product notes listed in [`onenote-notes.md`](onenote-notes.md) were unique
enough to bring over.

## Safety and provenance

One raw registration email contains a historical plaintext credential. It is intentionally not
copied into any Markdown conversion. Payment data, unsubscribe links, coupon codes, and personal
contact details that add no substantive guidance are also omitted. The raw sources remain local and
authoritative.
