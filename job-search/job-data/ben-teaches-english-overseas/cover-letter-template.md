## Template instructions

> Dear Hiring Manager,
>
> To begin your cover letter, include both the employer's and your contact information.
>
> In the first paragraph of your cover letter, begin by telling the employer the position you are
> applying for and how you learned about the opportunity.
>
> The rest of this paragraph should briefly present basic information about yourself, including
> your degree, area of study or expertise, and career goals as they align with the company's goals.
>
> The second paragraph should respond directly to the job description written by the hiring
> manager. Describe how your previous job experiences, skills, and abilities will allow you to meet
> the company's needs. To make that easier, you can and should include relevant words and phrases
> from the job description in your cover letter.
>
> To go the extra mile, research the company and what it is doing in the current state of its
> industry. In an optional third paragraph, explain how you can contribute to that work and help the
> company achieve its goals.
>
> The final paragraph is the call-to-action portion. Say that you would like to interview, state that
> you will follow up in a week if you do not hear back, and thank the reader for their time.
>
> Sincerely,
> Chibuzor Ejimofor

The wording above is lightly normalized for encoding and punctuation; its paragraph structure and
instructions are unchanged.
