# "What budget are you comfortable managing in a calendar year?"

## Say it like this

Honestly it depends a bit on how the portfolio's set up, but I can give you a sense of where I sit. On a single project I've owned a buildout end to end, so the budget, the buyout, the cost tracking, all of it. Across a region with a few projects running at the same time, I'd be comfortable carrying somewhere in the range of ten to twenty million in total project value over a year. I'd actually want to turn it around and ask what range this role typically carries, so I can speak to it more precisely.

## Notes

- They want a portfolio number (across multiple projects in a year), not a single-project number. Think in aggregate annual value.
- Flipping the question back ("what range does this role carry?") shows you think at the program level and keeps you from lowballing or overshooting blind.
- Don't pair this with a specific square footage on the spot.
