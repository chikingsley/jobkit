# "What's your opinion on site safety?"

## Say it like this

For me safety comes before schedule, full stop. On my projects I walk the site on a standing checklist, and safety's the first thing on it, before I even look at progress. So I'm checking that everyone's got the right PPE, that we're operating to OSHA, that the stormwater controls are in place. I'm also a big believer in stop-work authority. Anybody on that site can stop work for an unsafe condition, and there's no penalty for it. And honestly a safe site ends up being a productive site anyway, because an incident is about the most expensive hit your schedule can take.

## Notes

- Lead with the value (safety before schedule), back it with a real habit (your standing safety walk), and close on the business case (incidents wreck schedules).
- Stop-work authority is the phrase that signals a mature safety culture.
