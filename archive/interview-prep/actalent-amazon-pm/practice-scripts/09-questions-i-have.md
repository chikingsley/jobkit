# "What questions do you have?"

Always have three or four ready. These double as a way to surface what you care about (travel, ramp).

## Ask these

1. What does the project pipeline look like over the next year? New builds, retrofits, or a mix, and what's the typical project size?
2. How's the design relationship set up? Do I partner with in-house architects, or external firms project by project?
3. You mentioned aligning projects regionally with possible national travel down the line. What's the realistic travel expectation in the first six to twelve months?
4. You said you're building out the training program right now. What does ramp-up look like for someone starting now with projects ready to go?

## Notes

- Question 3 matters to you personally (travel vs. your own plans). Ask it warmly, as interest, but listen hard to the answer before this goes further.
- Questions 1 and 4 mirror back what the recruiter already told you, which shows you were listening.
