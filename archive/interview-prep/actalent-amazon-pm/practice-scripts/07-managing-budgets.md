# "How do you feel about managing budgets?"

## Say it like this

I'm comfortable with it, it's a core part of the job for me. The way I run it, I'm tracking committed cost against the forecast the whole time, and I'm tight on change orders, so any scope change gets priced and approved before the work goes ahead. And if I see a budget risk coming, I'd much rather flag it early than surprise an owner with it late. On my projects I've used Kahua to track the cost and the invoices.

## Notes

- This is an attitude check. Be affirmatively comfortable, then show a system (committed vs. forecast, change-order discipline, early flagging).
- Ties to the spend S-curve idea from the readiness answer if they push further.
