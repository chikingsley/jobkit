# "Tell me about your partnership with architects."

## Say it like this

Yeah, I've worked closely with architects. As an owner's rep I'm usually working from the design set into permitting and then out into the field, so I sit right in the middle of that relationship. The way I approach it is collaborative. I want to catch constructability issues early, keep the RFIs tight and moving, and protect the design intent all the way through to the field. On my Pfizer retrofit, for example, I produced the existing-conditions survey that the whole design baseline was built on, and then I was the one feeding field reality back to the design team as we went.

## Notes

- "Partnership with architects" = can you work the design side, not just the field side. Design reviews, RFIs, submittals, resolving design-vs-field conflicts without it turning into a fight.
- The architect is the "Architect of Record." If they ask how you handle disagreements with the designer, frame it as catching it early and solving it together, not winning.
